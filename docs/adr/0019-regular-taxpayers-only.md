# billow serves regular taxpayers, not composition dealers

A Business is either GST registered as a regular taxpayer, issuing Tax
invoices, or not GST registered, issuing Bills of supply. A composition
dealer is neither, and billow does not serve one.

## Considered Options

A composition dealer holds a GSTIN yet may not charge tax. It must issue Bills
of supply carrying "composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on
supplies", and it pays tax on turnover in CMP-08 rather than per invoice.
Treating GST registered as "holds a GSTIN" would make billow issue such a
dealer Tax invoices charging GST, which the dealer is forbidden to collect.
Supporting it properly means a third kind of Business, a different document
and different returns, for a Business billow does not currently serve.

## Consequences

"GST registered" in billow means a regular taxpayer. Adding composition later
is a new registration type on the Business and a new document rule, not a
reinterpretation of the flag. Until then, the absence should stay visible
rather than be approximated.
