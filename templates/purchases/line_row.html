{% comment %} combobox/remote spells the floor and the debounce as an htmx trigger filter, `input[this.value.length > 1] changed delay:300ms`. htmx compiles that filter with new Function, which `script-src 'self'` refuses, and the floor then never applies at all. So the picker dispatches `lookup` itself, having done the floor, the debounce and the same-query check in linePicker; htmx still owns the request and the swap, and `hx-sync this:replace` still drops a request in flight so two keystrokes cannot settle on the older reply. Named `q`, as the directory's search is. A bill with ten lines posts ten of them and the form has no such field, so Django ignores them. {% endcomment %}
